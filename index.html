The AI Pipeline

How I make games without a team · AI-directed development

My approach

I design, art-direct and playtest. AI does the heavy production work and I check everything it makes.

  1. Concept ComfyUI image generation for keys, palettes and zones
  2. Characters style sheets, then Meshy and Blender for 3D models and rigs
  3. Motion AI text-to-motion and phone-video mocap
  4. Code AI coding agents build what I spec; I playtest and tune
  5. Ship Windows, Mac and browser builds on itch.io
